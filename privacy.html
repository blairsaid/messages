Privacy policy

Messages · effective 26 September 2026

Messages is a personal Android app used only by its owner, on the owner's own phone. It is not distributed to anyone else. This policy describes what the app does with data.

Messages on the phone

Text messages, photos, videos and contacts stay on the owner's phone, in the app's private storage and Android's own message store. The app has no server and sends none of this anywhere, apart from the messages the owner chooses to send and the HD sharing described below.

Google Drive (HD sharing)

When the owner turns on HD sharing, full-quality copies of photos and videos they send are uploaded to a Google Drive account the owner controls. The app requests only the drive.file permission, which limits it to files it has created itself; it cannot read, change or delete anything else in that Drive or Google account.

Each uploaded file is shared as "anyone with the link can view", and that link is sent in the message. Anyone the link reaches can view the file. Location data is removed from photos before upload.

The Google sign-in is handled by Google Play services on the phone. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings, under third-party connections.

What is not collected

The app does not collect analytics, does not show advertising, and does not sell, rent or share data with anyone. It uses Google user data only to upload and share the files described above, and does not transfer it to third parties or use it for any other purpose.
