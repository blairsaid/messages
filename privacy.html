Privacy policy

Messages · effective 26 September 2026

Messages is a personal Android app used only by its owner, on the owner's own phone. It is not distributed to anyone else. This policy describes what the app does with data.

Messages on the phone

Text messages, photos, videos and contacts stay on the owner's phone, in the app's private storage and Android's own message store. The app has no server and sends none of this anywhere, apart from the messages the owner chooses to send and the HD sharing described below.

HD sharing

When the owner turns on HD sharing, full-quality copies of photos and videos they send are uploaded to a Cloudflare R2 storage bucket the owner controls. Each message's files sit under a long random address, and a link to them is sent in the message. Anyone the link reaches can view the files; they cannot be found or listed without it. Location data is removed from photos and videos before upload. The owner deletes old uploads from the app's settings, or automatically after a set age.

The gallery page that shows them (on this site) holds no photos itself: it loads each file straight from the bucket, and the part of the link that says which files never reaches this site.

What is not collected

The app does not collect analytics, does not show advertising, and does not sell, rent or share data with anyone. It does not use any Google account data.
