Messages

A personal SMS and MMS app for Android.

Messages is a text messaging app built by one person for their own phone. It is not published on any app store and is not offered to anyone else.

Its optional HD sharing feature uploads full-quality copies of photos and videos the owner sends to a storage bucket the owner controls, and puts one link to them in the message, because MMS can only carry heavily compressed media. The link opens a gallery page on this site.
